Community Rules (EULA)

Bevvy has live, per-bar chat. By using Bevvy you agree to a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive behavior. You will not post content that is unlawful, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, or that bullies or impersonates anyone.

You can report any message (press and hold it → Report) and block any user (press and hold → Block). Reported content is automatically hidden once multiple people flag it, and we remove offending content and may eject abusive users. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours. There is no tolerance for abusive users or objectionable material.

Privacy Policy

What we collect, and why:

  • Account: your email and a username, to sign you in.
  • Location: your approximate location, only while the app is open, to show nearby bars and to confirm you're at a bar before you post in its chat. We do not track your location in the background or sell it. You can hide your presence entirely with Ghost mode (Profile → Ghost mode).
  • Activity: the bars you save, and your presence at a bar while the app is open, so others can see how busy it is right now. Live crowd counts are only shown once several people are present, so an individual is never singled out.
  • Chat: messages you send in a bar's chat, visible to others in that chat.

We use Supabase (our hosting/database provider) to store this data. We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account, which removes your profile, saved bars, presence, and messages.
